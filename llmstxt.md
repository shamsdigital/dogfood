## Services
- Custom home construction: site evaluation before land purchase, RightStart preconstruction, design-partner coordination, construction, and a tiered warranty. Custom homes in this market commonly land around $350 per square foot, depending on finishes, land, and site conditions.

- RightStart preconstruction: site evaluation, feasibility, scope, budget, and schedule settled in writing before construction begins.
