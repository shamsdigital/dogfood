Stellan Eoin Builders is a licensed Tennessee general contractor (BC 73150) building event venues, gathering spaces, and community and faith based facilities across Nashville and Middle Tennessee. Assembly spaces combine three things most commercial projects never face at once: large spans, heavy occupant loads, and dates that do not move.

## What is different about building an assembly space?

The building is designed around a crowd. Occupant load drives nearly every major decision: how many exits the space needs and how wide they are, what the fire protection and alarm systems must handle, how the mechanical system keeps a full room comfortable, and what the structure spans so the floor plan stays open. A venue that seats a few hundred people is not an office with fewer walls. It is a different building, reviewed under a different occupancy classification, and designing to that reality from the start is considerably cheaper than converting to it later.

Then there is the calendar. A venue books revenue before it opens. Weddings get deposits, seasons get sold, and a community facility promises its congregation or its neighborhood a date. A venue that misses its season is not behind schedule. It is out a year. We manage these projects with that math in front of us.

## What does Stellan Eoin build in this category?

Event venues. Wedding and private event spaces where the open span, the finishes, and the back of house flow all have to serve a room full of guests at once.

Gathering and community spaces. Multipurpose facilities, community buildings, and civic gathering rooms built for heavy, varied use.

Faith based facilities. Sanctuaries and ministry buildings, where assembly occupancy requirements meet a congregation's budget and timeline, and where trust in the builder matters as much as the bid.

We work alongside your design team from feasibility. Large span structures and high occupant loads reward early cost and constructability feedback, while the drawings are still flexible, so the design that gets finished is one that can be built for the budget it was designed to.

## How do you protect an opening date that is already booked?

We schedule backward from it. Float goes where the risk actually sits: long lead structural components, fire protection and alarm inspections, and the trades that cannot start until something else finishes. Life safety inspections on an assembly space are not a formality, and a certificate of occupancy timeline gets planned like any other trade, not hoped for at the end. If anything threatens the date, you hear about it early rather than at the end.

We have worked with permitting departments in more than twenty Tennessee jurisdictions, and assembly projects are where that fluency pays most, because occupancy reviews and life safety requirements are exactly where jurisdictions differ.

## Who is accountable for the work?

We are a general contractor. We do not self-perform. We select, contract, and manage licensed trade partners, and we answer for every one of them. Our founder has been building hands-on since 1993, before ever managing a project, which means the person accountable for your building knows what the work in front of him should look like, not just what the schedule says. And because we also manage custom home projects, our commercial finish work holds to a residential standard of detail. In a venue, the finishes are part of what the client is renting.

## Questions we hear most

**What does occupant load mean for my project budget?**
More than most owners expect. Occupant load sets the exit count and widths, the fire protection scope, the restroom counts, and the mechanical capacity, and each of those is a budget line. We establish the intended occupancy early in RightStart preconstruction so those costs are in the number from the beginning rather than surfacing as change orders.

**Can you build a venue while we are booking events?**
The honest answer is that construction and a booked calendar have to be planned together. If an existing space is operating, we plan phasing, hours, and containment around it. If the venue is new, we build the schedule backward from the first booked date and tell you plainly whether that date is realistic before you sell it.

**Do you build churches and ministry facilities?**
Yes. Faith based buildings are assembly occupancy projects with the same code obligations as any venue, usually carried by a budget a congregation raised. We price honestly, plan carefully, and treat that money with the respect it took to gather.

**How large a span can you build?**
Span is a structural design question we work through with your design team, and the answer depends on the system, the site, and the budget. Our Tennessee BC license is the state's broadest building classification, covering the structural range this work requires.

**Are you licensed and insured for assembly projects?**
Yes. Tennessee Contractor License BC 73150 and Metro Nashville and Davidson County Master Contractor License MCN151894, both publicly verifiable. We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage and are happy to provide certificates on request.
